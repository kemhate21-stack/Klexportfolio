About

Operations first, then the tools.

Glad you’re here. I’ve spent the last four years learning how businesses really run—and where work tends to break. I started in customer service and operations, moved into social media, and eventually found my focus in building systems that make businesses run more smoothly.

I’ve managed five clients under one agency, building pipelines and workflows around each client’s specific needs—from organizing processes to creating automations that reduce repetitive work and keep things moving. My approach combines operations, data analysis, social media, and automation to build systems that are practical, not just complicated.

I’ve also helped grow a small fast-food brand’s social presence by generating 100K additional views and 200 new followers.

Based in Manila, I work with clients across different time zones, building systems that help them spend less time remembering what needs to be done—and more time getting things done.
